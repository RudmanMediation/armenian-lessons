# Lesson 12 — Western Armenian (Beirut)

How to read this script:
- **N:** the English narrator.
- **M:** (Aram, the man) or **W:** (Sona, the woman) speaks Armenian. Each line shows how it sounds, then the Armenian letters for the speakers, then the meaning.
- **(pause)** is your turn to say it out loud before you hear the answer.

---

## Part 1 — Opening conversation

N: This is Lesson 12 of Western Armenian. Listen to this conversation. Aram and Sona are at a café.

M: Sona, inch guzes khemel? — Սօնա, ի՞նչ կ'ուզես խմել։ — *Sona, what do you want to drink?*
W: Surj me guzem. Tun? — Սուրճ մը կ'ուզեմ։ Դո՞ւն։ — *I want a coffee. You?*
M: Yes al surj guzem. — Ես ալ սուրճ կ'ուզեմ։ — *I want coffee too.*
M: Yergu surj, khntrem. Kani é? — Երկու սուրճ, խնդրեմ։ Քանի՞ է։ — *Two coffees, please. How much is it?*
W: Dasnergu dolar. — Տասներկու տոլար։ — *Twelve dollars.*
M: Dasnergu dolar? Shad sugh é! — Տասներկու տոլա՞ր։ Շատ սուղ է։ — *Twelve dollars? That's very expensive!*
W: Voch, sugh ché. Hos amen pan sugh é. — Ոչ, սուղ չէ։ Հոս ամէն բան սուղ է։ — *No, it's not expensive. Everything here is expensive.*

---

## Part 2 — "Coffee," "too," "please"

N: Aram asked Sona what she wanted to drink. Ask your friend, "What do you want to drink?" (pause)
M: Inch guzes khemel? — Ի՞նչ կ'ուզես խմել։
N: Sona wanted coffee. The word for "coffee" is…
W: surj — սուրճ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: urj (pause) … surj (pause) … surj (pause)
N: Say, "I want a coffee." Remember "a" or "one" comes after the word: "coffee a." (pause)
W: Surj me guzem. — Սուրճ մը կ'ուզեմ։
N: Now ask your friend, "Do you want a coffee?" (pause)
W: Surj me guzes? — Սուրճ մը կ'ուզե՞ս։
N: Aram said he wanted coffee too. "Too" or "also" is…
M: al — ալ
N: "Me too" is…
M: Yes al. — Ես ալ։
N: Repeat. (pause)
M: Yes al.
N: Say, "I want coffee too." (pause)
M: Yes al surj guzem. — Ես ալ սուրճ կ'ուզեմ։
N: Your friend says she wants to eat. Tell her, "Me too." (pause)
M: Yes al.
N: Aram ordered two coffees. You know "two." Say it. (pause)
M: yergu — երկու
N: Say, "two coffees." Armenian doesn't add an "s" after a number. (pause)
M: yergu surj — երկու սուրճ
N: To be polite when you order, you add "please."
M: khntrem — խնդրեմ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: trem (pause) … khntrem (pause) … khntrem (pause)
N: Order two coffees, please. (pause)
M: Yergu surj, khntrem. — Երկու սուրճ, խնդրեմ։
N: Now order one coffee, please. (pause)
M: Meg surj, khntrem. — Մէկ սուրճ, խնդրեմ։

## Part 3 — Numbers from ten to twenty

N: You know "ten." Say it. (pause)
W: dase — տասը
N: Armenian builds eleven to nineteen from "ten" plus the number. Eleven is "ten-one."
W: dasnemeg — տասնըմէկ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: meg (pause) … dasnemeg (pause)
N: "Twelve" is "ten-two."
W: dasnergu — տասներկու
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: dasnergu
N: How do you say "two"? (pause)
M: yergu
N: And "twelve"? (pause)
W: dasnergu
N: Now "fifteen." You know "five."
W: dasnehink — տասնըհինգ
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: dasnehink
N: Try "eighteen." (pause)
M: dasneut — տասնըութ
N: "Twenty" is a new word.
W: ksan — քսան
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: ksan
N: Say, "Twelve dollars." (pause)
W: Dasnergu dolar. — Տասներկու տոլար։
N: Say, "Twenty dollars." (pause)
M: Ksan dolar. — Քսան տոլար։
N: Ask, "How much is it?" (pause)
M: Kani é?
N: Answer, "Fifteen dollars." (pause)
W: Dasnehink dolar. — Տասնըհինգ տոլար։

---

## Part 4 — "Expensive" and "cheap"

N: Aram thought twelve dollars was a lot. He said, "It's very expensive." "Expensive" is…
M: sugh — սուղ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: ugh (pause) … sugh (pause) … sugh (pause)
N: Say, "It's expensive." (pause)
M: Sugh é. — Սուղ է։
N: You know "very" from "shad lav," "very good." Say, "It's very expensive." (pause)
M: Shad sugh é. — Շատ սուղ է։
N: Sona said, "No, it's not expensive." (pause)
W: Voch, sugh ché. — Ոչ, սուղ չէ։
N: "Cheap" is…
W: azhan — աժան
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: zhan (pause) … azhan (pause) … azhan (pause)
N: Say, "It's cheap." (pause)
W: Azhan é. — Աժան է։
N: Ask, "Is it cheap?" (pause)
M: Azhan é? — Աժա՞ն է։
N: Tell your friend, "No, it's very expensive. Twenty dollars." (pause)
W: Voch, shad sugh é. Ksan dolar. — Ոչ, շատ սուղ է։ Քսան տոլար։
N: Sona said everything here is expensive. "Everything" is…
W: amen pan — ամէն բան
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: amen pan
N: You know "pan" from "pan me," "something." Say, "Everything is expensive." (pause)
W: Amen pan sugh é. — Ամէն բան սուղ է։
N: Now, "Everything here is expensive." (pause)
W: Hos amen pan sugh é. — Հոս ամէն բան սուղ է։

---

## Part 5 — Review

N: Ask your friend, "Do you want to eat together?" (pause)
M: Guzes miasin udel? — Կ'ուզե՞ս միասին ուտել։
N: Tell her, "Yes, but later." Use "hedo" for "later." (pause)
W: Ayo, payts hedo. — Այո, բայց յետոյ։
N: "But" is "payts." Repeat. (pause)
W: payts — բայց
N: Ask her, "Is the restaurant far?" (pause)
M: Jasharane heru é? — Ճաշարանը հեռո՞ւ է։
N: Tell him, "No, it's close. There, on the right." (pause)
W: Voch, mod é. Hon, ach. — Ոչ, մօտ է։ Հոն, աջ։
N: Ask, "Do you have money?" (pause)
W: Tram unis? — Դրամ ունի՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes, I have twenty dollars." (pause)
M: Ayo, ksan dolar unim. — Այո, քսան տոլար ունիմ։
N: Ask your friend, "Coffee or wine?" Use "te" for "or" in a question. (pause)
M: Surj, te kini? — Սուրճ, թէ գինի՞։
N: Answer, "Wine, please." (pause)
W: Kini, khntrem. — Գինի, խնդրեմ։
N: Ask, "At what time?" (pause)
M: Zhame kaniin?
N: Answer, "At eight. Is that okay?" (pause)
W: Zhame utin. Lav é? — Ժամը ութին։ Լա՞ւ է։
N: Tell her, "Yes, very good." (pause)
M: Ayo, shad lav. — Այո, շատ լաւ։

### Notes for the speakers

Please say every line the way you would naturally say it to a friend in Beirut Armenian. If something sounds wrong or stiff, correct it. In particular:
- For "please" when ordering, do you say **khntrem**, or something else?
- For "expensive," is it **sugh**, or do you say it another way?
- Is **Hos amen pan sugh é** how you would say "everything here is expensive"?
