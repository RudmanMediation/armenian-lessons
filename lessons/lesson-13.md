# Lesson 13 — Western Armenian (Beirut)

How to read this script:
- **N:** the English narrator.
- **M:** (Aram, the man) or **W:** (Sona, the woman) speaks Armenian. Each line shows how it sounds, then the Armenian letters for the speakers, then the meaning.
- **(pause)** is your turn to say it out loud before you hear the answer.

---

## Part 1 — Opening conversation

N: This is Lesson 13 of Western Armenian. Listen to this conversation. Aram meets Sona in the street.

M: Parev Sona. Ur gertas? — Բարեւ Սօնա։ Ո՞ւր կ'երթաս։ — *Hi Sona. Where are you going?*
W: Dun gertam. Tun? — Տուն կ'երթամ։ Դո՞ւն։ — *I'm going home. You?*
M: Ararad jasharane gertam. Hedes ge kas? — Արարատ ճաշարանը կ'երթամ։ Հետս կու գա՞ս։ — *I'm going to the Ararat restaurant. Will you come with me?*
W: Ays kisher? — Այս գիշե՞ր։ — *Tonight?*
M: Ayo, ays kisher, zhame utin. — Այո, այս գիշեր, ժամը ութին։ — *Yes, tonight, at eight.*
W: Lav. Jasharane ur é? — Լաւ։ Ճաշարանը ո՞ւր է։ — *Okay. Where's the restaurant?*
M: Shidag gertas, hedo tsakh. — Շիտակ կ'երթաս, յետոյ ձախ։ — *You go straight, then left.*
W: Shad lav. Ertank! — Շատ լաւ։ Երթանք։ — *Very good. Let's go!*

## Part 2 — "Going"

N: Aram asked Sona where she was going. Listen.
M: Ur gertas? — Ո՞ւր կ'երթաս։
N: "You're going," to a friend, is…
M: gertas — կ'երթաս
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: tas (pause) … gertas (pause) … gertas (pause)
N: Ask your friend, "Where are you going?" (pause)
M: Ur gertas?
N: "I'm going" is…
W: gertam — կ'երթամ
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: gertam
N: Sona said, "I'm going home." "Home" is…
W: dun — տուն
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: dun
N: In Armenian you just say "home I'm going." Say, "I'm going home." (pause)
W: Dun gertam. — Տուն կ'երթամ։
N: Tell your friend, "I'm going to the Ararat restaurant." (pause)
M: Ararad jasharane gertam. — Արարատ ճաշարանը կ'երթամ։
N: Ask your friend, "Are you going home?" (pause)
M: Dun gertas? — Տուն կ'երթա՞ս։
N: Answer, "No, not me." (pause)
W: Voch, voch yes. — Ոչ, ոչ ես։

---

## Part 3 — "Come with me"

N: Aram asked Sona to come with him. You know "with me." Say it. (pause)
M: hedes — հետս
N: "You come," or "you'll come," to a friend, is…
M: ge kas — կու գաս
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: kas (pause) … ge kas (pause)
N: Ask your friend, "Will you come with me?" (pause)
M: Hedes ge kas? — Հետս կու գա՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes, I'll come." "I'll come" is "ge kam." (pause)
W: Ayo, ge kam. — Այո, կու գամ։
N: Repeat "I'll come." (pause)
W: ge kam — կու գամ
N: Ask your friend, "Will you come together?" (pause)
M: Miasin ge kas? — Միասին կու գա՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes, but later." (pause)
W: Ayo, payts hedo. — Այո, բայց յետոյ։

---

## Part 4 — "Tonight"

N: Sona asked, "Tonight?" "Tonight" is…
W: ays kisher — այս գիշեր
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: kisher (pause) … ays kisher (pause) … ays kisher (pause)
N: "Ays" means "this," and "kisher" means "night." Ask, "Tonight?" (pause)
W: Ays kisher?
N: Answer, "Yes, tonight, at eight." (pause)
M: Ayo, ays kisher, zhame utin. — Այո, այս գիշեր, ժամը ութին։
N: Ask your friend, "Do you want to eat together tonight?" (pause)
M: Ays kisher miasin udel guzes? — Այս գիշեր միասին ուտել կ'ուզե՞ս։
N: Answer, "Not tonight. Tomorrow." You know "tomorrow." (pause)
W: Ays kisher voch. Vaghe. — Այս գիշեր ոչ։ Վաղը։
N: Ask, "Tomorrow at what time?" (pause)
M: Vaghe zhame kaniin? — Վաղը ժամը քանիի՞ն։
N: Answer, "At nine." (pause)
W: Zhame innin. — Ժամը իննին։

---

## Part 5 — "Straight" and "then"

N: Sona asked where the restaurant was. Ask, "Where's the restaurant?" (pause)
W: Jasharane ur é? — Ճաշարանը ո՞ւր է։
N: Aram said, "You go straight, then left." "Straight" is…
M: shidag — շիտակ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: dag (pause) … shidag (pause) … shidag (pause)
N: Say, "You go straight." (pause)
M: Shidag gertas. — Շիտակ կ'երթաս։
N: You know "hedo" means "later." It also means "then," as in "and then." Say, "Straight, then left." (pause)
M: Shidag, hedo tsakh. — Շիտակ, յետոյ ձախ։
N: Now say, "You go straight, then right." (pause)
M: Shidag gertas, hedo ach. — Շիտակ կ'երթաս, յետոյ աջ։
N: Your friend asks if the restaurant is far. Tell her, "No, it's close. Straight, then left." (pause)
M: Voch, mod é. Shidag, hedo tsakh. — Ոչ, մօտ է։ Շիտակ, յետոյ ձախ։

---

## Part 6 — "Let's go"

N: At the end, Sona said, "Let's go!"
W: Ertank! — Երթանք։
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: tank (pause) … ertank (pause) … Ertank! (pause)
N: Say, "Let's go together." (pause)
W: Miasin ertank. — Միասին երթանք։
N: Say, "Let's go home." (pause)
M: Dun ertank. — Տուն երթանք։
N: Say, "Okay, let's go." (pause)
W: Lav, ertank. — Լաւ, երթանք։

---

## Part 7 — Review

N: Ask your friend, "How much is a coffee?" (pause)
M: Surj me kani é? — Սուրճ մը քանի՞ է։
N: Answer, "Fifteen dollars." (pause)
W: Dasnehink dolar. — Տասնըհինգ տոլար։
N: Say, "That's very expensive!" (pause)
M: Shad sugh é! — Շատ սուղ է։
N: Ask, "Where are you going?" (pause)
W: Ur gertas?
N: Answer, "I'm going home. Will you come with me?" (pause)
M: Dun gertam. Hedes ge kas? — Տուն կ'երթամ։ Հետս կու գա՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes. Let's go together." (pause)
W: Ayo. Miasin ertank. — Այո։ Միասին երթանք։

### Notes for the speakers

Please say every line the way you would naturally say it to a friend in Beirut Armenian. If something sounds wrong or stiff, correct it. In particular:
- For "straight ahead," do you say **shidag**, or something else?
- Is **Hedes ge kas?** how you'd ask "Will you come with me?"
- For "tonight," is it **ays kisher**, or **ays irigun** (this evening)?
