# Lesson 11 — Western Armenian (Beirut)

How to read this script:
- **N:** the English narrator.
- **M:** (Aram, the man) or **W:** (Sona, the woman) speaks Armenian. Each line shows how it sounds, then the Armenian letters for the speakers, then the meaning.
- **(pause)** is your turn to say it out loud before you hear the answer.

---

## Part 1 — Opening conversation

N: This is Lesson 11 of Western Armenian. Listen to this conversation. Aram and Sona are friends, and they're deciding where to eat.

M: Sona, guzes miasin udel? — Սօնա, կ'ուզե՞ս միասին ուտել։ — *Sona, do you want to eat together?*
W: Ayo, guzem. Ur? — Այո, կ'ուզեմ։ Ո՞ւր։ — *Yes, I want to. Where?*
M: Jasharan me, hos mod. — Ճաշարան մը, հոս մօտ։ — *A restaurant, near here.*
W: Heru ché? — Հեռու չէ՞։ — *Isn't it far?*
M: Voch, heru ché. Mod é. Hon, tsakh. — Ոչ, հեռու չէ։ Մօտ է։ Հոն, ձախ։ — *No, it's not far. It's close. There, on the left.*
W: Kani é? — Քանի՞ է։ — *How much is it?*
M: Dase dolar. — Տասը տոլար։ — *Ten dollars.*
W: Tram unis? — Դրամ ունի՞ս։ — *Do you have money?*
M: Ayo, tram unim. — Այո, դրամ ունիմ։ — *Yes, I have money.*

## Part 2 — "Together," "near," "far"

N: Aram asked Sona if she wanted to eat together. Listen.
M: Guzes miasin udel? — Կ'ուզե՞ս միասին ուտել։
N: The word for "together" is…
M: miasin — միասին
N: Repeat each part after him, starting from the end.
M: sin (pause) … asin (pause) … miasin (pause) … miasin (pause)
N: Ask your friend, "Do you want to eat together?" (pause)
M: Guzes miasin udel? — Կ'ուզե՞ս միասին ուտել։
N: Say it again. (pause)
M: Guzes miasin udel?
N: Now ask, "Do you want to drink together?" (pause)
W: Guzes miasin khemel? — Կ'ուզե՞ս միասին խմել։
N: Answer, "Yes, I want to." (pause)
W: Ayo, guzem. — Այո, կ'ուզեմ։
N: Sona asked "Where?" How do you say "Where?" (pause)
W: Ur? — Ո՞ւր։
N: Do you remember how to say "here"? (pause)
M: hos — հոս
N: "Near" or "close" is…
M: mod — մօտ
N: Repeat. (pause)
M: mod
N: To say "near here," Armenian puts "here" first: "here near." Listen and repeat.
M: hos mod (pause) … hos mod (pause)
N: Say, "A restaurant, near here." (pause)
M: Jasharan me, hos mod. — Ճաշարան մը, հոս մօտ։
N: Sona asked, "Isn't it far?" The word for "far" is…
W: heru — հեռու
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: ru (pause) … heru (pause) … heru (pause)
N: How do you say "It's far"? (pause)
W: Heru é. — Հեռու է։
N: And "It's not far." (pause)
M: Heru ché. — Հեռու չէ։
N: Now "It's close." (pause)
M: Mod é. — Մօտ է։
N: Ask your friend, "Is it far?" (pause)
W: Heru é? — Հեռու է՞։
N: Tell her, "No, it's not far. It's close." (pause)
M: Voch, heru ché. Mod é. — Ոչ, հեռու չէ։ Մօտ է։

## Part 3 — "Left" and "right"

N: Aram told Sona where the restaurant was: "There, on the left." You know "there." Say it. (pause)
M: hon — հոն
N: "Left" is…
M: tsakh — ձախ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: akh (pause) … tsakh (pause) … tsakh (pause)
N: Say, "There, on the left." (pause)
M: Hon, tsakh. — Հոն, ձախ։
N: "Right," as in the direction, is…
W: ach — աջ
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: ach (pause)
N: Say, "There, on the right." (pause)
W: Hon, ach. — Հոն, աջ։
N: You know how to say "the Ararat restaurant." Say it. (pause)
M: Ararad jasharane — Արարատ ճաշարանը
N: Ask, "Where is the Ararat restaurant?" (pause)
W: Ararad jasharane ur é? — Արարատ ճաշարանը ո՞ւր է։
N: Tell her, "It's close, on the left." (pause)
M: Mod é, tsakh. — Մօտ է, ձախ։
N: She asks you again. Answer, "There, on the right." (pause)
M: Hon, ach. — Հոն, աջ։
N: How do you say "left"? (pause)
M: tsakh
N: And "right"? (pause)
W: ach

## Part 4 — "How much?" and money

N: Sona asked, "How much is it?" Listen.
W: Kani é? — Քանի՞ է։
N: You've heard "kani" before, in "zhame kaniin," "at what time?" "Kani" means "how much" or "how many." Repeat each part.
W: ni (pause) … kani (pause) … Kani é? (pause)
N: Ask, "How much is it?" (pause)
W: Kani é?
N: Aram said, "Ten dollars." The word for "ten" is…
M: dase — տասը
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: se (pause) … dase (pause)
N: "Dollar" is…
M: dolar — տոլար
N: Armenian doesn't add an "s" after a number. Say, "ten dollars." (pause)
M: Dase dolar. — Տասը տոլար։
N: Say, "Five dollars." (pause)
M: Hink dolar. — Հինգ տոլար։
N: Now "Eight dollars." (pause)
W: Ut dolar. — Ութ տոլար։
N: Ask, "How much is it?" (pause)
W: Kani é?
N: Answer, "Nine dollars." (pause)
M: Inn dolar. — Ինը տոլար։
N: The word for "money" is…
W: tram — դրամ
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
W: am (pause) … tram (pause) … tram (pause)
N: Ask your friend, "Do you have money?" (pause)
W: Tram unis? — Դրամ ունի՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes, I have money." (pause)
M: Ayo, tram unim. — Այո, դրամ ունիմ։
N: Now say, "I don't have money." (pause)
M: Tram chunim. — Դրամ չունիմ։
N: Ask your friend, "How much money do you have?" (pause)
W: Kani tram unis? — Քանի՞ դրամ ունիս։
N: Tell her, "Ten dollars." (pause)
M: Dase dolar.

## Part 5 — Talking to a friend

N: In earlier lessons you learned to speak politely, the way you'd talk to an elder, a stranger, or a group. With a friend, Armenians talk more casually. Listen to how you ask a friend "How are you?"
M: Inchbés es? — Ինչպէ՞ս ես։
N: Repeat. (pause)
M: Inchbés es?
N: Answer, "I'm fine." (pause)
W: Lav em. — Լաւ եմ։
N: To a friend, "you" is…
W: tun — դուն
N: Repeat. (pause)
W: tun
N: Say, "I'm fine. And you?" (pause)
W: Lav em. Tun? — Լաւ եմ։ Դուն՞։
N: Ask your friend, "Do you understand?" (pause)
M: Ge hasgenas? — Կը հասկնա՞ս։
N: Answer, "Yes, I understand a little." (pause)
W: Ayo, kich me ge hasgenam. — Այո, քիչ մը կը հասկնամ։
N: Ask your friend, "What do you want to eat?" (pause)
W: Inch guzes udel? — Ի՞նչ կ'ուզես ուտել։
N: You learned "Esek," "Tell me," the polite way. To a friend, you say…
M: Esé — Ըսէ
N: Repeat. (pause)
M: Esé
N: Say to your friend, "Tell me, how much is it?" (pause)
M: Esé, kani é? — Ըսէ, քանի՞ է։

## Part 6 — At what time?

N: You know how to ask "At what time?" Ask it. (pause)
W: Zhame kaniin? — Ժամը քանիի՞ն։
N: Here's how to say "we will eat."
M: bidi udenk — պիտի ուտենք
N: Repeat each part, starting from the end.
M: denk (pause) … udenk (pause) … bidi udenk (pause)
N: Ask Sona, "At what time will we eat together?" (pause)
M: Zhame kaniin bidi udenk miasin? — Ժամը քանիի՞ն պիտի ուտենք միասին։
N: She says, "At eight."
W: Zhame utin. — Ժամը ութին։
N: Say, "At eight or at nine?" (pause)
M: Zhame utin, gam innin? — Ժամը ութին, կամ իննի՞ն։
N: Ask her, "Do you want to eat with me at eight?" (pause)
M: Guzes zhame utin hedes udel? — Կ'ուզե՞ս ժամը ութին հետս ուտել։
N: Answer, "Yes, I want to. Where?" (pause)
W: Ayo, guzem. Ur? — Այո, կ'ուզեմ։ Ո՞ւր։
N: Tell her, "The Ararat restaurant. It's close." (pause)
M: Ararad jasharane. Mod é. — Արարատ ճաշարանը։ Մօտ է։

### Notes for the speakers

Please say every line the way you would naturally say it to a friend in Beirut Armenian. If something sounds wrong or stiff, correct it. In particular:
- For "money," do you say **tram**, or the everyday **para**?
- For "How much is it?", is it **Kani é?** or **Inch kin é?**
- For "on the left" and "on the right," would you say just **tsakh** and **ach**, or something like **tsakh goghme**?
